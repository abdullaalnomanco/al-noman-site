Overview

Before stepping into design leadership, I spent two years building core UI/UX craft across three roles — plus a parallel year as a Research Assistant, which turned out to matter more than I expected.

Devlab Solution — UI/UX Designer (Jun 2023 – Dec 2024)

Wireframing, prototyping, and interaction design across multiple end-to-end projects, run concurrently with continuous user testing to catch problems before they reached production.

Beeo Digital — Intern, UX/UI Designer (May – Dec 2022)

My first real exposure to structured user research — learning to actually ask users what they needed rather than assuming, and iterating designs based on what came back.

Corporate Ask — Intern, Graphic Designer (Jan – Jun 2022)

My first professional design role. Foundational, entry-level work that taught the basics of designing for a client rather than for a coursework brief.

FabLab, Daffodil International University — Research Assistant (Feb 2022 – Feb 2023)

Running in parallel with the internships above, this role supported research activities and project work at DIU's FabLab — an early, informal preview of the research track I'd later pursue formally at UCA.
