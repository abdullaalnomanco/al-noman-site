========================================================================

random respondent from condition B, on the same scenario. Whoever made the
correct decision on that scenario wins the duel; both-correct/both-wrong
ties split 0.5/0.5. This uses each person's actual outcome, not the group
mean, so a 'weaker' condition still wins plenty of individual duels.
