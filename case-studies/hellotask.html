Practice · Current Role

Head of Design, Hellotask Technologies

Jan 2025 — PresentDesign Leadership

Overview

My current role, leading design at Hellotask Technologies Ltd. It's the first position where I own the full design function end to end, rather than executing within someone else's process.

What the role covers

  • End-to-end design process — from early research and ideation through to final UI/UX execution.
  • Cross-functional alignment — working directly with engineering, product, and marketing to keep design decisions tied to actual company objectives, not design-for-design's-sake.
  • User research & usability testing — keeping every decision grounded in what real users need, not internal assumptions.
  • Design systems — establishing and maintaining a scalable, consistent system rather than one-off screens.
  • Mentoring — guiding the design team and building a culture of continuous improvement.

Why it matters

This role runs in parallel with my MSc research — the design-system and mentoring work here is the practical counterweight to the more theoretical trust-calibration research I do at UCA. Each track sharpens the other.
