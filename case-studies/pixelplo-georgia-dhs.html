Practice · Senior Visualizer, PixelPlo

Georgia DHS Document Digitization & Search Solution

Jan — Dec 2024Employee of the MonthSole Designer

Overview

As Senior Visualizer at PixelPlo, I was the sole designer on the Georgia DHS Document Digitization & Search Solution — built in collaboration with EBIW on a developer-heavy team where design decisions had to be made fast and defended clearly.

What I designed

  • Natural-language search — letting caseworkers query documents in plain English rather than learning a query syntax.
  • OTP-protected downloads — balancing document security requirements with a download flow that didn't feel bureaucratic.
  • OCR & vectorization upload flows — designing the upload experience around a genuinely technical backend process, so the complexity stayed invisible to the end user.

Process

Being the only designer on a government-facing platform meant conducting user research directly with DHS managers, then translating those findings into interfaces without a design team to sanity-check against — every decision had to be justified on its own merits.

Outcome

The project was recognized internally — I was named Employee of the Month at PixelPlo in 2024, largely on the strength of this work.
