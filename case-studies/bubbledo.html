Research · UCA Coursework Project (FGCT7022)

BubbleDo — Spatial Task Management

SUS 84.0 — ExcellentReact prototype10-participant study

Overview

BubbleDo rethinks the task manager as a spatial environment rather than a list. Tasks appear as floating bubbles, with urgency encoded through colour, pulse speed, and an SVG ring countdown — rather than through sort order or a due-date column.

Evolution

The concept moved through several forms before landing here: it started as a VR room-scale idea, passed through a mixed-reality phase, and was finally built as a browser-based prototype (with development support from a collaborator, Sam) so it could actually be usability-tested within the project timeline.

Testing & findings

  • Tested with 10 participants; scored a mean System Usability Scale of 84.0 — the "Excellent" band.
  • The standout finding wasn't something I designed for: 9 of 10 participants spontaneously grouped bubbles spatially without being instructed to — suggesting spatial organization is a more natural mental model for task urgency than list order.
  • Two qualitative evaluators (UCA Master's students) independently rated the concept 5/5.

What's next

The roadmap includes undo support, a completed-tasks archive, a proper onboarding flow, and — eventually — a return to the original MR/VR concept now that the interaction model is validated in 2D.
