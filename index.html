Research Interests

The questions I keep coming back to — across design and research.
01

Human-Computer Interaction

How people actually use systems, versus how systems are designed to be used.

02

Human-AI Interaction

The interface layer where people and AI systems negotiate control, judgment, and understanding.

03

Trustworthy & Explainable AI

Designing explanations that calibrate trust, rather than just increasing it.
